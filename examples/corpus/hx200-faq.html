HX-200 frequently asked questions

Can I verify the calibrator myself?

Yes. Verification against a reference multimeter is allowed and described in chapter 3 of the maintenance manual. Adjustment is not: it must be done by an accredited laboratory, otherwise the calibration certificate is no longer valid.

How long does the warm-up take?

Forty-five minutes. Readings taken earlier can drift by several microvolts on the 100 mV range.

Which firmware version do I have?

Open Settings, then About. Versions before 3.2.0 do not report the warning W-212 and should be updated with the tool on the support page.

What does error E-415 mean?

The contact resistance of the output relay is above its limit. Order relay RLY-4471-B and follow section 4 of the manual, or book a service visit.

Where do I find my serial number?

On the label under the instrument and in Settings, About. It starts with HX2 followed by seven digits, for example HX20481736.
